Breakdown questions

What should I do while I wait for the recovery truck?

Put your hazard lights on and wear a hi-vis vest if you have one. On a motorway or fast road, leave the car by the passenger side and wait behind the barrier, well away from traffic. If you are stuck in a live lane and can't get out safely, call 999 first.

What do I tell you when I call?

Where you are (road, town, junction or the number on the nearest marker post), which car or van it is and what happened. Not sure where you are? Name a shop, petrol station or house number nearby.
