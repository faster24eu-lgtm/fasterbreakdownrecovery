Privacy Policy

How Faster Breakdown Recovery collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Faster Breakdown Recovery collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. We keep this deliberately simple: this is a small local recovery business, and we only collect what's needed to respond to your enquiry.

What we collect

This website does not use a database, and it does not track visitors automatically. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to give us directly, such as through our contact and quote form or by phone, email or WhatsApp. This may include your name, phone number, email address, vehicle make and model, and your location or a description of what's happened.

The quote form on this site is currently set up to open your own email application with your details pre-filled, so that submission is sent directly from your device to ours as a normal email — it is not stored on this website or in any separate database run by us.

How we use it

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry — to understand what's happened, arrange recovery, and, where needed, follow up about the job. We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to deal with your request and any related follow-up, such as an invoice query. We don't keep personal data longer than necessary, and we periodically remove information that's no longer needed.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for full detail.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it if it's wrong, and to ask us to delete it. If you'd like to exercise any of these rights, or have any questions about how your information is handled, contact us at info@fasterbreakdownrecovery.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the way we handle information changes — for example, if we start using a proper form backend or add analytics — we'll update this page to reflect that.

Questions About Your Data?

Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.
