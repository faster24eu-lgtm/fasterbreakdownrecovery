Breakdown Recovery on the M40

The M40 is the main alternative to the M1 between London and Birmingham, running through the Chilterns, past Oxford and on to Warwick. If you've broken down anywhere along it, call us with the nearest junction and your direction.

From £125 · 24/7 · often with you in about 30 minutes (depends on location and traffic)

London to the Chilterns

The M40 begins at Denham on the western edge of London, crosses the M25 at junction 16 and passes Beaconsfield and High Wycombe before climbing through the Chiltern Hills at the Stokenchurch Gap, where the motorway runs through a deep cutting. Beaconsfield services (junction 2) is the first stop out of London.

Oxford, Bicester and Banbury

Beyond the Chilterns the M40 passes Oxford (junctions 8 and 9, with Oxford services at junction 8a), Bicester Village (junction 9), which draws heavy weekend traffic, and Banbury. Cherwell Valley services sits near junction 10. Junctions are well spaced here, so the driver location signs and the services are the easiest way to tell us where you are.

Warwick and the M42

The northern section runs past Warwick and Leamington, with Warwick services between junctions 12 and 13, and joins the M42 south of Birmingham at junction 3a. Traffic merging towards Birmingham and the M6 can be heavy at peak times.

If you break down on the M40: get onto the hard shoulder or as far left as you can, hazards on, and if it's safe get out by the left-hand door and wait behind the barrier. Then call us with the junctions either side of you and your direction.

M40 Breakdown FAQs

How do I tell you where I am on the M40?

Give us the last junction or services you passed, your direction (towards London or Birmingham), and the number on the nearest blue driver location sign if you can see one.

Can you take my car home to London or Birmingham?

Yes. Long-distance recovery is no problem. Tell us the destination and we'll include it in the fixed price.

How much does recovery from the M40 cost?

Local recovery starts from £125, with a fixed price agreed before we set off.
