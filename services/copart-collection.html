Copart collection · UK-wide

Copart Collection & Delivery Across the UK

Won a car at a Copart auction? We collect it from the yard, whether it runs, rolls or is accident-damaged, and deliver it to your home, garage, workshop or port. Book quickly, before storage charges start, and get a fixed price upfront. Local jobs from £125.

Call Now WhatsApp Us

Why buyers use us

Salvage auction cars are often non-runners, accident-damaged or missing keys, and the yard expects them to be collected promptly once paid for. After the free period, storage charges start adding up every day. We book the collection quickly, bring the right equipment for a damaged car, and deliver it where you need it, so you can concentrate on the repair or the resale.

What we collect

  • Non-runners, including cars with engine, gearbox or electrical faults
  • Accident-damaged cars (Cat S and Cat N), including cars that don't roll or steer properly
  • Cars without keys, with the right loading equipment
  • Vans and light commercials up to 3.5 tonnes
  • Runners that you'd rather not drive home without insurance, tax or an MOT

Copart yards we collect from

We collect from every Copart UK yard, including:

  • South East: Sandwich (Kent), Rochford (Essex), Colchester (Dedham, Essex, off the A12)
  • East of England: Wisbech (Cambridgeshire, on the A47), Sandy and Wootton (Bedfordshire, near the A1 and the M1)
  • South and South West: Newbury (Greenham, Berkshire, near the M4 and A34), Westbury (Wiltshire), Bristol (Avonmouth, by the M5 and M49)
  • Midlands and North West: Wolverhampton (Wombourne), Chester (Sandycroft, Deeside)
  • North of England: Sandtoft (North Lincolnshire, near Doncaster and Scunthorpe), Peterlee (County Durham, near the A19)
  • Scotland: Whitburn (West Lothian, near the M8)
  • Northern Ireland: Belfast (Saintfield, County Down)

Copart opens and expands yards regularly, so if yours isn't listed, just tell us which yard the lot is at.

What we need from you

  1. The Copart yard the vehicle is at, and the lot number
  2. The make and model, and whether it runs, rolls and has keys (photos from the listing help)
  3. Confirmation that the car is paid for and ready for collection, plus any collection reference or authority the yard needs from you
  4. The delivery address: your home, a garage, a workshop, a storage unit or a port

Yards have their own collection hours and procedures, which can change, so check the current rules for your yard on your Copart account. We'll work around them.

Beat the storage clock: send us the details as soon as you've won and paid. The sooner the collection is booked, the less you risk paying daily storage.

Several cars, or regular buying?

If you buy regularly or have several cars to move at once, tell us. Multiple collections from the same yard can often be planned together, which keeps the cost per car down.

Exporting a salvage car?

Buying at Copart UK for export? We can deliver to a UK port or, on request, arrange transport to the continent. See our European transport and ask us for a price.

Questions

Copart Collection FAQs

Can you collect a non-runner from Copart?

Yes, that's most of what we collect. Tell us whether it rolls and steers and whether there are keys, so we bring the right equipment.

How quickly can you collect from a Copart yard?

It depends on the yard's collection hours and our availability in that area. Send us the details as soon as you've paid and we'll give you a collection time with the quote.

How much does a Copart collection cost?

Local collections start from £125. The price depends mainly on the distance to the delivery address and the vehicle's condition, and it's fixed before we book.

Do you collect from other salvage and car auctions too?

Yes. We also collect from other salvage auctions, trade auctions and dealers. Just tell us where the vehicle is.

Won a Car at Copart?

Send us the yard, the lot number, the vehicle and the delivery address. We’ll come back to you with a fixed price and a collection time.
