Recovery across the full range of vehicles

"Vehicle recovery" is the umbrella under which our other, more specific pages sit. If you drive a standard car, our car recovery page covers that in more detail; if you run a van for work, van recovery is written specifically with tradespeople in mind. This page is for anyone who wants the broader picture — how we handle whatever's parked on your drive or stopped at the roadside, whatever type of vehicle it is.

In practice that spans small hatchbacks and city cars, saloons and estates, 4x4s and SUVs, pickups, and vans and light commercial vehicles used for trade or business. Each has its own quirks when it comes to recovery, and we plan around them rather than treating every vehicle the same way. Coverage runs across Birmingham city and out into the West Midlands.

Why vehicle type changes the recovery method

A low-slung sports car needs different ramp angles and loading care than a jacked-up 4x4. A long-wheelbase panel van has a different centre of gravity and turning circle to a small car, which affects how it's positioned on a flatbed. Ground clearance, overall weight, whether the vehicle is four-wheel drive, and how it's loaded internally all factor into whether we tow, winch onto a flatbed, or use wheel-lift equipment. Getting this right matters both for the safety of the recovery and for avoiding further damage to your vehicle.

  • Standard cars, estates and saloons.
  • 4x4s, SUVs and pickups.
  • Vans and light commercial vehicles.
  • Vehicles that aren't safe to drive due to a fault, damage, or being a non-runner.

Tell us the details. When you call, let us know the vehicle type, roughly how big or heavy it is, and its condition. That's what lets us send the right recovery vehicle first time rather than having to reassess on arrival.

Questions

Vehicle Recovery FAQs

What types of vehicle can you recover?

We recover cars, 4x4s, vans and other light commercial vehicles. Tell us the make, model and roughly how the vehicle is loaded (or if it's not loaded at all) when you call, so the right recovery vehicle and equipment can be sent.

Can you recover a 4x4 or larger vehicle?

Yes. 4x4s and larger cars often need different handling than a standard hatchback because of their weight, ground clearance and sometimes four-wheel-drive systems, and we take that into account when planning the recovery.

Is there a weight limit?

It depends on the vehicle being recovered. Tell us the vehicle type and approximate weight when you call, particularly for larger vans or heavily loaded vehicles, so we can confirm suitability before dispatch.
