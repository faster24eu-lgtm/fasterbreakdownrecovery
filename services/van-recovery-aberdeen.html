Van recovery

Van Recovery Aberdeen

A van off the road is lost working time. We recover vans and light commercial vehicles across Aberdeen and Aberdeenshire, taking them straight to a garage or depot of your choosing.

Call Now Get a Quote

Built for tradespeople and small businesses

When a car breaks down it's an inconvenience. When a van breaks down, it's often a day's earnings or a missed job. We understand that a van being off the road affects your work, not just your commute, so the aim is always to get the vehicle collected and moved on with as little disruption as possible.

This covers panel vans, small tradesperson's vans and other light commercial vehicles used for work. If you're driving a standard car rather than a van, our general car recovery page is likely a better fit, and if you'd like the wider picture across every vehicle type we handle, see vehicle recovery.

Recovery to the destination that suits you

Rather than defaulting to a single depot, we'll take your van wherever you need it — your regular garage, a dealership, your own premises, or storage. Tell us the address when you call and that's where the van goes, so you can keep working with people you already trust rather than starting again with someone unfamiliar.

  • Recovery of vans and light commercial vehicles.
  • Taken to a garage, depot or address of your choice.
  • Handled with tools, equipment or stock left inside.
  • A single point of contact throughout the recovery.

A word on contents. If your van is carrying tools, stock or equipment, we'll recover it as it stands, but we'd recommend removing anything particularly valuable or easily lost before we arrive if you're able to. We can't be responsible for loose items during transport, so it's worth a quick check if you have the time.

When you need us

Whether it's a mechanical fault, a flat battery that won't take a jump, or a van that simply won't start on a job site, get in touch and describe the situation. We'll talk through where the van is, where it needs to go, and get a recovery vehicle moving.

Questions

Van Recovery FAQs

Can you recover a van with tools or stock inside?

Yes, the van is recovered as it is. We'd recommend removing anything particularly valuable or easily lost beforehand if you're able to, simply because we can't be responsible for loose contents during transport.

Do you recover larger panel vans as well as small ones?

We handle a range of van sizes. Tell us the make, model and roughly how large or heavily loaded the van is when you call, so we can confirm suitability and send the right equipment.

Can you take my van straight to my usual garage?

Yes. Just give us the address when you call and we'll take the van there directly, rather than to a default location, so you can keep working with the garage you already trust.
