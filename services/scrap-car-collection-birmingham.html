Scrap car collection

Scrap Car Collection Birmingham

Got a car that's finished, whatever state it's in? We collect scrap cars and non-runners free of charge, anywhere across Birmingham and the West Midlands.

Call Now WhatsApp Us

Same-day collection where possible, depending on your area and availability — ask when you call.

Free collection, any condition

It doesn't matter what state the car is in — running or not, damaged, rusted through, failed its last MOT, or simply sat on the drive unused for years. If it's ready to go, we'll collect it, and there's no charge for the collection itself. This covers cars of any make and model across Birmingham and the wider West Midlands.

How it works

Call or WhatsApp us with the vehicle's make and model, its location, and roughly what condition it's in — whether it starts and moves under its own power, or needs to be loaded. We'll arrange a collection time, and where possible we can often collect the same day, depending on your area and how busy we are. Have the keys ready, along with the vehicle's log book (V5C) if you can find it, since that helps the process go smoothly.

  • Non-runners and cars that won't start.
  • Cars that have failed their MOT.
  • Accident-damaged vehicles no longer worth repairing.
  • Old cars that have sat unused on a driveway or in a garage for years.
  • Any make or model — we don't turn cars away for being too old, too rusty or too far gone.

Where your car goes

Once collected, your vehicle is taken on to be dealt with properly and in line with the relevant regulations for end-of-life vehicles. If you need documentation or more detail for your own records, ask us when you book collection and we'll confirm exactly what applies to your vehicle.

Before we collect. Please remove any personal belongings from the car beforehand if you're able to — we can't be responsible for anything left inside once it's collected. If you have the V5C log book, having it ready speeds things up, but let us know if you can't locate it and we'll talk through the options.

Not sure this is the right service?

If your car has broken down and you need it recovered rather than scrapped, our breakdown recovery or general car recovery pages cover that. If you're planning a non-emergency move of a car that still has some life in it — to storage, a new owner, or another address — see car transport instead.

Questions

Scrap Car Collection FAQs

Do you charge for scrap car collection?

No — collection is free, anywhere across Birmingham and the West Midlands.

Does my car need to be running or roadworthy?

No. We collect non-runners, cars that have failed their MOT, accident-damaged vehicles and cars that have simply sat unused for years, in any condition.

What do I need to have ready when you collect the car?

The vehicle's keys, and its logbook (V5C) or registration details if you have them, help the process go smoothly. If you can't find the logbook, tell us when you book and we'll talk through the options.
