Van recovery · UK-wide

Van Recovery Across the UK, 24/7

When your van stops, so does your work. We recover panel vans, Luton vans, crew cabs and other light commercial vehicles anywhere in the UK, loaded or empty, day or night. From £125, with a fixed price agreed upfront.

Call Now WhatsApp Us

Vans of every size

From small car-derived vans to long-wheelbase panel vans like the Sprinter, Transit and Crafter, Luton box vans and tippers. We recover light commercial vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes. When you call, give us the make, model and wheelbase, so we send a truck that can actually carry it.

Loaded vans

A van full of tools, stock or materials weighs a lot more than an empty one, and that matters for the recovery vehicle. Tell us roughly what's on board and how heavy it is. In some cases it's better to recover the van with the load. In others, part of the load needs to come out first. We'll tell you honestly before we set off.

Back to work as fast as possible

We take the van wherever helps you most: your usual garage, a dealer that can fix it quickly, your yard or depot, or home. If you're mid-job, tell us. Sometimes the best plan is to drop you and your tools at the job before taking the van to the garage.

Broken down on a site or in a loading bay? Tell us about the access: gates, height barriers, soft ground or tight turns. Building sites and industrial estates are fine, as long as a recovery truck can get in safely.

Motorways and long distances

Vans cover a lot of motorway miles, and many of our van recoveries come from the M1, M6 and M25. We can also bring a van home over a long distance if you'd rather your own garage fixed it.

Questions

Van Recovery FAQs

Can you recover a van that's fully loaded?

Often, yes, depending on the van and the weight of the load. Tell us what's on board and roughly how heavy it is, and we'll tell you whether it can be recovered loaded.

Do you recover vans over 3.5 tonnes?

We recover light commercial vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes. For heavier vehicles, call us and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How much does van recovery cost?

Local van recovery starts from £125. The price depends on the size and weight of the van, the load and the distance, and it's agreed before anyone sets off.
