Car transport · UK-wide

Car Transport Anywhere in the UK

Bought a car at auction or online, moving house, or sending a car to a specialist at the other end of the country? We move running and non-running vehicles anywhere in the UK, on a date that suits you, for a fixed price agreed upfront. Local moves start from £125.

Call Now WhatsApp Us

Planned, not panicked

Car transport is for moves you can plan: no breakdown, just a vehicle that needs to get from A to B without being driven. You choose the date, we agree a fixed price, and the car is collected and delivered on a recovery truck.

What people use it for

  • Auction and online purchases: collected from the auction house, dealer or private seller and delivered to your door. Bought at a salvage auction? See Copart collection.
  • Selling a car: delivering it to a buyer anywhere in the UK.
  • Non-runners and projects: cars that don't run, have no MOT or are being restored.
  • Classic and prestige cars: moved without adding miles or risk.
  • House moves and storage: getting a second car to a new address or into storage.
  • Garages and specialists: taking a car to a specialist that's too far to drive to, or back home after repair.

What we need for a quote

The collection and delivery addresses, the make and model, whether it runs, rolls and steers, any access restrictions at either end, and your preferred date. Photos help, especially for non-runners. With that, we give you a fixed price.

Buying from a dealer or auction? Make sure the car is paid for and released for collection, and tell us the name of the person we should ask for. Auction houses often have set collection times, so let us know those too.

To and from Europe

If the car is on the continent, for example a car you bought in Europe or one stuck there after a breakdown, see vehicle repatriation to the UK.

Questions

Car Transport FAQs

How much does car transport cost?

It depends mainly on the distance, the vehicle and whether it runs. Local moves start from £125, and every job gets a fixed price before it's booked.

Can you move a car that doesn't run?

Yes. Non-runners are common. Tell us whether it rolls and steers, and whether the keys are available.

Do I need to be there at collection and delivery?

Someone needs to hand over the keys and receive the car, but it doesn't have to be you, as long as we know who to ask for.

Need a Car Moved?

Send us the collection and delivery addresses, the vehicle and your preferred date, and we’ll come back to you with a fixed price.
