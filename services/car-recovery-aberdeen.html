What we mean by car recovery

Car recovery is a broader term than breakdown recovery. Our breakdown recovery page deals specifically with mechanical and electrical faults — the classic "it won't start" situation. Car recovery covers that and more: any scenario where your car needs to be collected and moved because it can't, or shouldn't, be driven. That includes mechanical faults, but also flat tyres with no usable spare, keys locked inside, cars that have picked up minor damage, and non-runners bought second-hand or at auction.

If your situation involves an actual road traffic collision rather than a mechanical problem, our dedicated accident recovery service is built around the extra care and safety steps that a crash scene needs.

Typical situations we're called out for

  • A flat battery that jump-starting hasn't fixed.
  • Keys locked inside the car with no spare set nearby.
  • A car that won't start at all, with no obvious cause.
  • Minor accident damage that leaves the car safe to load but not safe to drive.
  • A car bought at auction or privately that turns out not to run.

What to expect

When you call, we'll ask what's wrong, where the car is, and where you'd like it taken — a home address, a garage, a dealership, or storage. A recovery vehicle is sent out, the car is loaded using the appropriate method for its condition, and it's transported to your chosen destination. If the cause turns out to be something simple, like a battery that can be jump-started, we'll say so rather than automatically loading the car for the sake of it.

Safety first. If your car has stopped on a fast or busy road, don't wait inside it. Move yourself and any passengers well away from moving traffic, keep hazard lights on, and call us from a safe position.

Not sure which service you need?

If the problem is clearly mechanical — a fault that's developed while driving — our breakdown recovery page covers that specifically. If your car needs to be moved for a planned reason rather than an emergency, such as relocating a non-runner, take a look at car transport instead. If you're unsure, just call and describe the situation — we'll point you the right way.

Questions

Car Recovery FAQs

Do you recover cars that have been in a minor bump?

Yes, provided the car is safe to move using standard loading equipment. For anything more serious, or where the road is blocked or someone is injured, see our accident recovery service, which is set up specifically for collision situations.

Can you recover a car with a flat tyre and no spare?

Yes. If there's no usable spare and the tyre can't be repaired at the roadside, we'll recover the car on a flatbed or with wheel-lift equipment so it isn't driven or dragged on a damaged tyre.

Do I need to be present when the car is collected?

In most cases, yes, or you'll need to arrange for someone else to hand over the keys and confirm the destination. Let us know when you call if you won't be at the vehicle yourself, and we'll talk through the options.
