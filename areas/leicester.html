Breakdown Recovery in Leicester

Leicester sits right on the M1, with the M69 to Coventry joining at junction 21, two ring roads and one of the busiest retail parks in the Midlands. We arrange recovery anywhere in Leicester and Leicestershire, 24 hours a day.

Junction 21 and Fosse Park

Junction 21 of the M1 is where the M69 from Coventry joins, and it sits right next to Fosse Park and the Meridian leisure park. At weekends and in the run-up to Christmas, traffic here can back up onto the motorway. Leicester Forest East services, just north of the junction, is a useful landmark if you've broken down on the M1 nearby.

The inner and outer ring roads

The A594 inner ring road runs tightly around the city centre, past the station and the Highcross area, with plenty of lights and lane changes. The A563 outer ring road loops round the suburbs. If your car stops on either, hazards on, get to the pavement, and tell us the nearest junction or landmark: the station, the King Power Stadium or a supermarket all work.

Event days

Match days at the King Power Stadium and the Tigers' ground bring heavy traffic and closures. If you break down nearby on a match day, let us know when you call so we can plan the quickest way in.

On the M1 near Leicester? Large stretches are smart motorway. If your car is struggling, aim for an emergency area or the next junction. If you're stuck in a live lane, stay in the car with your seatbelt on and call 999.

Around Leicester

We cover the whole city and Leicestershire, including Oadby, Wigston, Braunstone, Birstall, Loughborough, Hinckley and Market Harborough. Call with your postcode or a nearby landmark.
