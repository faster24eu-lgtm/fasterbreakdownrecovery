Coverage beyond the towns

Aberdeen and Aberdeenshire are joined together by a fairly small number of key roads, and a lot of our call-outs happen on those roads rather than at a fixed address. If you've broken down between the towns listed above, it's often more useful to think in terms of the route you're on rather than the nearest place name.

We regularly attend breakdowns on the A90, the main artery running north–south through the region and linking Aberdeen to Stonehaven, Portlethen and Ellon; the A96, which runs north-west through Dyce and Inverurie towards Huntly and Elgin; and the AWPR (Aberdeen Western Peripheral Route), the bypass that rings the city and connects most of the other routes together. If you're stopped on one of these roads, tell us the direction of travel and the nearest junction or landmark and we'll work out the quickest way to reach you.
