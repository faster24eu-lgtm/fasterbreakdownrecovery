Breakdown Recovery in Portlethen

Portlethen sits just off the A90 between Aberdeen and Stonehaven — a coastal commuter town with its own industrial estate. We recover cars from the town's estates, its industrial units and the A90 itself.

From £125 · 24/7 · often with you in about 30 minutes (depends on location and traffic)

A commuter belt on the A90 south

A large share of Portlethen's residents commute along the A90 into Aberdeen each day, which means the town sees a steady rhythm of morning and evening traffic. Breakdowns here often follow that pattern — cars that struggle to start on a cold morning before a commute, or vehicles that develop a fault on the drive home. Whether you're stuck on your own street or just about to join the A90, we'll come to you.

Industrial estate call-outs

Portlethen also has its own industrial estate, and we're regularly called out to vans and cars parked or working there. As with any industrial or business site, the unit number or company name is useful information, since these estates can have several similar-looking rows of units.

Coastal weather

Being a coastal town on this stretch of the North Sea coast, Portlethen gets more of the wind and driving rain that roll in off the water than towns further inland, and that has a real effect on breakdown patterns — wet weather is hard on ageing batteries and electrics, and strong coastal winds can make it harder and less pleasant to wait safely by the roadside. If you're waiting for us in poor weather, get as far from the carriageway as you safely can.

Broken down heading onto the A90 from Portlethen? Let us know which slip road or junction you're nearest to and your direction of travel — Aberdeen-bound or Stonehaven-bound — so we can plan the quickest approach along a fast road.

Getting to you

Portlethen sits between two larger places — Aberdeen to the north and Stonehaven further south — which means a breakdown here can sometimes be described by drivers in terms of one or the other rather than the town itself. That's not a problem: whether you think of yourself as "just south of Aberdeen" or "just outside Stonehaven," we cover the whole stretch, including the town's residential streets, its own local shops and the roads connecting them back to the A90.
