Breakdown Recovery in Southampton

Southampton is a port city, and plenty of its breakdowns happen around the docks: cars that won't start after two weeks parked for a cruise, vans delivering to the terminals, and traffic heading for the M27 and M3. We arrange recovery anywhere in Southampton, 24 hours a day.

Cruise terminals and port parking

Southampton is the UK's busiest cruise port, and thousands of cars are left parked near the terminals for one or two weeks at a time. A car that's been standing that long, especially in cold weather or with an older battery, often won't start on the day you get home. Tell us which terminal or car park you're in and roughly where the car is. A jump start may be enough. If not, we'll recover it home or to your garage.

The Itchen Bridge and the city roads

The Itchen Bridge links the city centre with Woolston, and Redbridge, the A33 and the Western Approach carry traffic from the M271 and the west. Around the docks and the city centre there's a lot of port and delivery traffic. If you break down on the bridge, put your hazards on and stay safe until it's clear to move. Tell us which side you're nearer to.

The M27 and the M3

The M27 runs east to west along the north of the city, towards Portsmouth one way and the New Forest the other, and the M3 heads north towards Winchester and London. Long stretches are busy smart motorway. If your car is struggling, aim for the next junction or an emergency area. If you're stuck in a live lane, stay in the car with your seatbelt on and call 999.

Back from a cruise and the car won't start? Tell us the terminal, the car park and your bay if you know it. Sometimes a jump start is all it takes, and we'll tell you honestly if it's likely to work.

Around Southampton

We cover the whole city and the surrounding area, including Eastleigh, Totton, Hedge End, Chandler's Ford, Romsey and the edge of the New Forest, as well as Portsmouth along the M27.

Southampton Recovery FAQs

My car won't start after a cruise. Can you help in the port car park?

Yes. Tell us which terminal and car park, and where the car is parked. If a jump start is likely to work, we'll say so. If not, we'll recover the car home or to your garage.

Do you cover Portsmouth and the New Forest too?

Yes. We arrange recovery across south Hampshire, and we'll give you an honest arrival estimate when you call.

Can you take my car home if I live far from Southampton?

Yes. Long-distance recovery is no problem. Tell us the destination and we'll include it in the fixed price.
