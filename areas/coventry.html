Breakdown Recovery in Coventry

Coventry's ring road is famous for its tight junctions and very short slip roads, and the city is boxed in by the M6, M69, A45 and A46. We arrange recovery anywhere in Coventry and Warwickshire, 24 hours a day.

The ring road

Coventry's inner ring road circles the city centre on an elevated loop with junctions only a few hundred metres apart. Traffic joining and leaving has very little space to merge, so a breakdown here can quickly become dangerous. If your car starts to struggle, take the next exit if you can. If it stops on the ring road, put your hazards on straight away and, if it isn't safe to get out, stay in the car with your seatbelt on and call 999. Tell us which junction number you're near.

The A45, A46 and the motorways

The A45 runs east to west across the south of the city towards Birmingham Airport, the A46 forms an eastern bypass towards Warwick, and the M6 passes to the north with the M69 heading off towards Leicester. Corley services on the M6 is a useful landmark. On these fast dual carriageways, get as far left as you can and behind the barrier if it's safe.

Around the city

The Coventry Building Society Arena, the university campuses and the hospital at Walsgrave all bring heavy traffic at certain times. If you break down near one of them on a busy day, tell us so we can plan the way in.

Stuck on the ring road? Don't try to cross the carriageway on foot. Stay with the car if it isn't safe to get out, keep your hazards on, and call. We'll tell you the safest way to wait.

Around Coventry

We cover the whole city and the surrounding area, including Kenilworth, Warwick, Leamington Spa, Nuneaton, Bedworth and Rugby. Call with your postcode or a nearby landmark.

Coventry Recovery FAQs

I've broken down on the Coventry ring road. What should I do?

Put your hazards on immediately. The slip roads are very short, so if it isn't safe to get out, stay in the car with your seatbelt on and call 999. Then call us with the nearest junction number.

Do you cover Leamington Spa and Warwick?

Yes. We arrange recovery across Coventry and Warwickshire, and we'll give you an honest arrival estimate when you call.

Can you take my car to Birmingham?

Yes. Tell us the destination and we'll include it in the fixed price.
