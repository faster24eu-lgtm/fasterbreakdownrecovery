Areas covered

Breakdown Recovery in Bridge of Don

Home to the P&J Live events complex and a string of retail parks, Bridge of Don sits on the Ellon Road/A90 corridor heading north out of Aberdeen. We recover vehicles from its car parks, retail sites and the surrounding streets, whatever's brought a car to a stop.

Event traffic around P&J Live

When there's a concert, exhibition or conference on at P&J Live, the volume of traffic in and around Bridge of Don increases sharply, and car parks fill up fast. A breakdown at these times is more disruptive than usual, both for you and for other vehicles trying to get in or out, so if your car won't move after an event, let us know it's an event night — we'll factor the extra traffic into how we plan getting to you and clearing the vehicle.

Retail park recovery

Bridge of Don has a significant concentration of retail parks, and a fair share of our call-outs here happen in supermarket or retail car parks — flat batteries after a short shopping trip, or a car that won't start after sitting for a while. These sites are usually straightforward for us to reach, but multi-level car parks or busy weekend car parks can still need some care, so tell us which store or unit you're nearest to.

On the Ellon Road corridor

The A90 runs through Bridge of Don as it heads north towards Ellon and beyond, and this stretch carries a steady flow of both local and longer-distance traffic. If you've broken down on this section of road rather than in a car park, your direction of travel and the nearest roundabout or junction help us get to you as directly as possible.

Broken down after a show or match at P&J Live? If you can, wait somewhere well clear of the exit routes other vehicles are using — a quiet corner of the car park rather than blocking a lane. Call us with the venue and roughly which car park you're in, and we'll work around the event traffic.

We'll always give you an honest estimate over the phone. Event traffic can slow things down, so let us know straight away if there's a show or exhibition on — it helps us plan the best route in.

My car won't start in a supermarket car park — is that something you cover?

Yes — retail park car parks are one of the most common places we're called to in Bridge of Don. Tell us which store you're near and we'll get someone out to you.
