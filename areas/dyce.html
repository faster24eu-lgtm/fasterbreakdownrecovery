Breakdown Recovery in Dyce

Dyce is Aberdeen's gateway — home to the airport and a concentration of energy-sector offices and industrial estates. We recover vehicles from the airport, its car parks, and the surrounding business parks, as well as the roads and estates around them.

Airport breakdowns and drop-offs

A breakdown at Aberdeen International Airport brings its own pressure — you might be trying to catch a flight, drop someone off, or pick them up, and the last thing you need is a car that won't move in a busy set-down area or car park. We understand the time-sensitive nature of airport call-outs and prioritise getting your vehicle out of the way and to wherever it needs to go, whether that's long-stay parking, a garage, or home.

A business-park landscape

Beyond the terminal, Dyce is one of Aberdeen's main business districts, with energy-sector offices and industrial estates spread across the area. Car parks here can be large, with multiple access points and site-specific rules, so if you break down on a business park it helps to give us the site name as well as the nearest building or unit number. Many of these breakdowns happen during the working week, often with a vehicle needed back on the road quickly for the next shift or meeting.

On the A96 corridor

Dyce sits along the A96, the main road linking Aberdeen to Inverurie, Huntly and beyond, and a fair number of the breakdowns we attend here happen on or just off that route rather than in the town itself. If you've broken down on the A96 near Dyce, let us know your direction of travel and the nearest junction — the road carries a steady flow of traffic in both directions and getting the approach right matters.

Dropping off or collecting from the airport? If your vehicle fails in a set-down zone or short-stay area, move it as far out of the traffic flow as you safely can, put your hazards on, and call us straight away — these areas need to keep moving and we'll aim to clear you quickly.

Questions

Dyce Recovery FAQs

Can you recover a car from inside the airport grounds?

Yes. Let us know which car park or set-down area you're in, and whether the vehicle can be moved a short distance — even a few metres into a bay clear of traffic makes a big difference to how quickly we can work.

My van has broken down on a business park in Dyce — can you help mid-week?

Yes — we cover Dyce's business and industrial parks throughout the week. Give us the site name and unit number if you have it, along with a contact number, and we'll get a vehicle out to you.
