Breakdown Recovery in Solihull

Solihull is Birmingham's gateway — home to Birmingham Airport, the NEC and a concentration of business parks along the M42. We recover vehicles from the airport, its car parks, and the surrounding business parks, as well as the roads and estates around them.

Airport breakdowns and drop-offs

A breakdown at Birmingham Airport brings its own pressure — you might be trying to catch a flight, drop someone off, or pick them up, and the last thing you need is a car that won't move in a busy set-down area or car park. We understand the time-sensitive nature of airport call-outs and prioritise getting your vehicle out of the way and to wherever it needs to go, whether that's long-stay parking, a garage, or home.

A business-park and exhibition landscape

Beyond the terminal, Solihull is one of the region's main business districts, with office parks and logistics sites spread across the borough alongside the NEC's exhibition and events traffic. Car parks here can be large, with multiple access points and site-specific rules, so if you break down on a business park it helps to give us the site name as well as the nearest building or unit number. Many of these breakdowns happen during the working week, often with a vehicle needed back on the road quickly for the next shift or meeting — and when there's a major show on at the NEC, traffic in the area can build quickly.

On the M42 corridor

Solihull sits directly on the M42, the motorway linking Birmingham to Erdington's side of the conurbation and on towards the M6 and M40, and a fair number of the breakdowns we attend here happen on or just off that route rather than in the town itself. If you've broken down on the M42 near Solihull, let us know your direction of travel and the nearest junction — the road carries a steady flow of traffic in both directions and getting the approach right matters.

Dropping off or collecting from the airport? If your vehicle fails in a set-down zone or short-stay area, move it as far out of the traffic flow as you safely can, put your hazards on, and call us straight away — these areas need to keep moving and we'll aim to clear you quickly.

Solihull Recovery FAQs

Can you recover a car from inside the airport grounds?

Yes. Let us know which car park or set-down area you're in, and whether the vehicle can be moved a short distance — even a few metres into a bay clear of traffic makes a big difference to how quickly we can work.

My van has broken down on a business park in Solihull — can you help mid-week?

Yes — we cover Solihull's business parks throughout the week. Give us the site name and unit number if you have it, along with a contact number, and we'll get a vehicle out to you.
