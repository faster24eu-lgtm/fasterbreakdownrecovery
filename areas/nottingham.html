Breakdown Recovery in Nottingham

Nottingham's traffic squeezes over the Trent on a handful of bridges, round a ring road and out to the M1 west of the city. We arrange recovery anywhere in Nottingham and Nottinghamshire, 24 hours a day.

From £125 · 24/7 · often with you in about 30 minutes (depends on location and traffic)

Crossing the Trent

Most traffic heading south out of Nottingham uses Clifton Bridge on the A52 ring road or Trent Bridge closer to the centre. Both are bottlenecks at peak times and on match and cricket days at Trent Bridge and the City Ground. If you break down on a bridge, put your hazards on, stay safe, and tell us which bridge and your direction.

The ring road and the trams

The A6514 ring road runs round the north and west of the city, with busy junctions at places like Basford and Bobbers Mill. The NET tram network runs on-street through parts of the centre, Hyson Green and Beeston. If you break down near tram lines, get well clear of the tracks and tell us the nearest tram stop. It helps the driver find you fast.

The M1, junctions 24 to 27

The M1 passes to the west of Nottingham, with junction 25 linking to the A52 into the city and Trowell services between junctions 25 and 26. East Midlands Airport is just south of junction 24. Much of this section is smart motorway, so watch for emergency areas and red X signs if your car starts to struggle.

City-centre multi-storeys: if your car won't start in a car park near the Victoria or Broadmarsh area, tell us the car park name, the level and the height limit at the entrance.

Around Nottingham

We cover the whole city and the surrounding area, including Beeston, West Bridgford, Arnold, Hucknall, Long Eaton, Mansfield and Newark. Call with your postcode or a nearby landmark.

Nottingham Recovery FAQs

I've broken down near a tram line in Nottingham. What should I do?

Get yourself and any passengers well away from the tracks and the road, put your hazards on, and call us with the street and the nearest tram stop.

Can you collect my car from East Midlands Airport?

Yes. Tell us which car park and roughly where the car is parked, and we'll arrange recovery.

Do you cover Mansfield and Newark too?

Yes. We arrange recovery across Nottinghamshire, and we'll give you an honest arrival estimate when you call.
