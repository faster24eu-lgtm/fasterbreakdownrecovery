Breakdown Recovery in Inverurie

Inverurie is the main market town of the Garioch, sitting on the A96 north-west of Aberdeen with its own railway station on the Aberdeen–Inverness line. We recover cars from the town centre, surrounding villages and the A96 itself.

Call Now Get a Quote

The A96 through the Garioch

Inverurie sits directly on the A96, the main road connecting Aberdeen to Huntly, Elgin and beyond, and a good number of the breakdowns we attend here happen on that road rather than within the town itself. Traffic on this stretch moves quickly and includes a mix of local commuters and longer-distance journeys, so if you've broken down on the A96 near Inverurie, your direction of travel and the nearest junction help us plan the safest way to reach you.

A market town at the centre of a rural area

Inverurie itself functions as the main town for a wide surrounding area of smaller villages and farmland in the Garioch. That means some of our call-outs are on quieter rural roads leading into town rather than on the A96 or in the town centre — roads that can be narrower, with fewer passing places and less passing traffic to flag down. If you're on one of these roads, a nearby village name or farm sign is genuinely useful information for us.

Rail and road together

With a station on the Aberdeen–Inverness line, a number of Inverurie residents combine rail travel with driving — parking at the station or picking someone up from it. A breakdown around the station car park or on the approach roads at peak commuting times can be more disruptive than usual, so let us know if that's your situation and we'll aim to get the vehicle clear as quickly as we can.

Broken down on a quiet rural road near Inverurie? These roads can be exposed with little passing traffic. Stay warm, keep your hazards on if it's safe to remain with the car, and give us the nearest village, farm or landmark rather than relying on a postcode alone.

Do you cover the villages around Inverurie, not just the town?

Yes, we cover the wider Garioch area around Inverurie. Tell us the nearest village or a recognisable landmark and we'll work out the best way to reach you.

My car won't start at the train station car park — can you help?

Yes — let us know it's the station car park and roughly whereabouts you're parked, and we'll get a vehicle out to you.
