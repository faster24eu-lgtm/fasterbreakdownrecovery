Breakdown Recovery in Bradford

Bradford sits in a bowl surrounded by hills, linked to the M62 by the short M606 spur. We arrange recovery anywhere in Bradford and the surrounding towns, 24 hours a day.

From £125 · 24/7 · often with you in about 30 minutes (depends on location and traffic)

The M606 and the M62

The M606 is a short motorway spur from junction 26 of the M62 (the Chain Bar interchange) up to the edge of the city. It's busy at peak times and ends at a large roundabout. Hartshead Moor services on the M62 is close by and a useful landmark. If you break down on the M606, get as far left as you can and behind the barrier if it's safe.

The inner ring road and city centre

The inner ring road runs round the city centre, with steep approaches from every direction, because Bradford is built on hillsides. Clutches, brakes and engines work hard here, especially in older cars and loaded vans. If you stop on a steep road, leave the car in gear (or Park) with the handbrake on and the wheels turned to the kerb.

The Clean Air Zone

Bradford has a Clean Air Zone covering the city centre and the main roads around it. Private cars aren't charged, but older non-compliant vans, taxis, buses and lorries are. It doesn't affect recovering a broken-down vehicle, but if you drive a van, tell us where it needs to go so we can plan around it.

Broken down on a hill? Tell us how steep the road is and whether the vehicle is likely to roll. It helps us send the right recovery truck first time.

Around Bradford

We cover the whole district and the surrounding area, including Shipley, Bingley, Keighley, Ilkley, Halifax and Huddersfield, as well as Leeds next door.

Questions

Bradford Recovery FAQs

Does the Bradford Clean Air Zone affect my car?

Private cars aren't charged. Older non-compliant vans, taxis, buses and lorries are. It doesn't affect recovering your vehicle.

I've broken down on the M606. What should I do?

Get as far left as you can, put your hazards on and, if it's safe, get out and behind the barrier. Then call us and tell us your direction of travel.

Do you cover Keighley and Halifax as well?

Yes. We arrange recovery across Bradford and West Yorkshire, and we'll give you an honest arrival estimate when you call.
